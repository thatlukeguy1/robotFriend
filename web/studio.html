Who is Bolt?

What she's called everywhere: the chat page, the Studio and her own replies. (Her wake word is separate.)
Her character: what she's like, what she cares about, what she knows about herself.
Her way of speaking: how long her answers are, how formal, any phrases she likes.
Protected rules (always added, can't be changed here)

These tell Bolt how to use its tools and that its words are spoken out loud. If your personality asks for something these rules forbid (like long lists), the rules win.
